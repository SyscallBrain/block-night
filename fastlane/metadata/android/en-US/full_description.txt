<p>Block Night is a falling-blocks game set in a city at night. Climb twelve districts of fifty floors each: every floor has its own goal, from clearing lines to lighting windows or digging through garbage, and a budget of pieces to do it.</p>

<p><b>Every floor can be cleared.</b> Each of the 1,800 variants ships with a solution certificate, found by an offline solver and replayed with the same engine that runs the game. A floor always deals the same pieces, so losing never changes your luck.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>600 floors, each tuned separately for Easy, Medium and Hard</li>
<li>Seven kinds of goals: lines, score, Quads, T-Spins, combos, lit windows and garbage</li>
<li>Concrete, rising garbage and floors without Hold</li>
<li>Daily challenge: one floor per day and difficulty</li>
<li>Free play: Marathon, Sprint 40, Ultra 2:00 and Zen</li>
<li>SRS rotation, 7-bag, Hold, ghost piece, T-Spins, back-to-back, combos and perfect clears</li>
<li>Gestures over the whole play area or an on-screen pad with adjustable DAS and ARR, both with a left-handed layout</li>
<li>Stars, statistics, records and achievements</li>
<li>Progress export and import through the system file picker</li>
<li>English and Portuguese</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.</p>
